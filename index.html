Problem

Applicant handling depended too heavily on manual inbox work, delayed responses, and disconnected tools. Tracking, follow-up, and stage visibility needed to be more consistent.

What I built
  • Auto-reply workflow for incoming applicant emails
  • Applicant pipeline sync across Airtable, ClickUp, and Kit
  • Stage-based follow-up automation
  • AI-assisted reply interpretation for incoming responses
  • Daily accountability and stage-tracking support workflows
  • Scheduled task orchestration to keep systems running consistently
How I approached it

I treated it as a systems problem, not a one-off automation problem. I mapped the flow from inbox to pipeline to follow-up, separated the tasks that needed automation from the tasks that needed structure, then built supporting workflows around reliability and visibility.

Business value

Faster response handling, clearer applicant tracking, reduced manual routing, and less day-to-day dependency on one person remembering every next step.
