About

The person behind the backend cleanup

I'm Audrey — a Backend Operations & Automation Support Specialist based in the Philippines with 14 years of experience in operations and quality assurance, including 13 years specifically in QA, audits, compliance, SOPs, and accuracy-focused execution.

I came up through real operational work — reviewing processes, catching errors, building SOPs, and managing the details that keep systems running. Over time, that QA foundation grew into backend systems support: cleaning up CRMs, troubleshooting automation failures, auditing lifecycle logic, and documenting workflows that nobody ever wrote down.

My work isn't development. I'm not building products from scratch. I'm the person who comes in when the systems that are supposed to be running aren't behaving — and I diagnose what's broken before fixing it, document what's unclear, and stabilize what's unreliable.

My approach starts with diagnosis, not changes. I review the current setup first, identify where the process is breaking, then recommend or apply fixes based on what the audit shows. This prevents rushed changes, duplicate work, and new issues caused by fixing the wrong part of the system.

Every cleanup includes documentation, root-cause notes, and a clear handoff — so the system stays maintainable after the fix, not just while I'm in it.

I take on contract and freelance engagements where backend reliability and operational clarity actually matter. I work well asynchronously — with clear updates, organized documentation, and reliable follow-through across different time zones.

Case Studies

Problems I've solved

Specific examples of backend issues I diagnosed, cleaned up, and stabilized for real client systems.

Automation Audit

Whop → Zapier → Kit Lifecycle Audit

A Whop-based membership system had a broken Invoice Paid filter, a hardcoded email address that should have been dynamic, and tag mismatches causing zero subscribers to enter the Churn Trial sequence. An API authorization error on one Zap had been silently failing with no one aware.

I traced each Zap from trigger to final action, identified every failure point, documented the fixes with step-by-step instructions, and prepared a full audit handoff so the client could take action without decoding the system from scratch.

Outcome: Zero subscribers entering the Churn Trial sequence identified as a logic error — not normal behavior. Documented fix path for all affected Zaps. Full handoff included so the client could execute independently.
Whop Zapier Kit / ConvertKit Lifecycle Audit Automation QA
CRM & Email Deliverability

Outbound Email Pipeline — Instantly + NeverBounce + Suppression

Leads from multiple sources needed a clean, trackable process before reaching the outbound tool. Without a defined pipeline, the risk was duplicate outreach, invalid emails, and poor deliverability damaging the sender reputation.

I built a Google Sheets control layer that acted as the source of truth, removed generic and unverifiable contacts, ran the list through NeverBounce, enforced a suppression list, and created a clear status flow from Verified → Queued → Sent. The process was documented as a repeatable workflow so future batches run the same validation without rebuilding from scratch.

Outcome: Clean, auditable lead pipeline. Duplicate and invalid contacts removed before reaching inboxes. Suppression logic and documented workflow hold across future batches.
Instantly NeverBounce Google Sheets Zoho CRM Suppression Logic

GoHighLevel CRM Audit & Workflow Structuring

Email Sequence Ops

Kit Sequence Stall — 226 Subscribers Stuck Mid-Sequence

Approximately 226 subscribers had stalled after Email 2 in a Kit sequence and weren't advancing. The issue wasn't a broken trigger — it was that repeated content edits had been resetting Kit's send timer, silently halting progress for everyone in that sequence.

I diagnosed the root cause by tracing the edit history and Kit's timer behavior, documented the fix, and resolved it without re-firing the earlier emails that subscribers had already received.

Outcome: Sequence restored for 226 subscribers. Root cause documented so it won't recur. Fix applied without re-triggering emails subscribers had already received.
